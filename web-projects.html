My Stuff

Web Projects

Digital media workflows, independent artist sites, and creative experiments on the web.

My work on the web sits at the intersection of technical problem-solving, visual performance, and clean design. By day, I work as a Staff Technical Customer Success Manager at Cloudinary, where I partner with digital teams, developers, and enterprise brands to help them get the most out of their visual media and digital asset workflows.

At its core, Cloudinary solves one of the modern web’s biggest bottlenecks: managing, transforming, and delivering vast libraries of images and videos quickly and reliably across every device and screen size. Instead of teams manually resizing files, juggling disconnected storage drives, or slowing down their websites with unoptimized media, Cloudinary centralizes digital asset management (DAM) and automates format conversion, compression, responsive cropping, and CDN delivery on the fly. I’m genuinely passionate about helping businesses succeed in this space because visual media is often the very first impression a brand makes—and when high-resolution storytelling loads instantaneously without sacrificing quality, both user experience and business performance thrive.

Outside of my day job, I love bringing that same appreciation for fast, thoughtful web craft to independent music and personal creative projects. Whether I am building and refining my own artist portfolio on GitHub Pages, experimenting with interactive 3D WebGL scenes like my Window Light Study, or working with WordPress and static site workflows, I enjoy turning ideas into lightweight, responsive experiences that feel intentional and effortless to explore.

One of my favorite spare-time projects was designing and launching a complete website makeover for a well-known and appreciated ambient musician, brought to my attention through a close friend and my wife's yoga playlists. As an admirer of his music, I took it on purely as an extracurricular labor of love—modernizing an older site into a clean, responsive single-page experience that lets his catalog, streaming links, and meditative aesthetic shine. Helping an independent artist achieve a digital home that truly reflects the warmth and depth of their work is one of the most rewarding ways to build for the web.
